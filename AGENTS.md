# Agent notes

Player-facing setup is in [README.md](README.md).

The solution is `Peak.Bazooka.slnx` and the C# namespace `Peak.Bazooka`. The assembly and BepInEx plugin id are `PeakBazooka`. The Thunderstore package name is `Bazooka`.

The gun is a clone of PEAK's AntiZooka. `Plugin.CreateFromAntiZooka` waits for the item catalog, clones that prefab, strips `Action_RaycastSpawnSomething` (the antigravity bubble), `Action_ReduceUses`, and `Antigrav` (so a dropped bazooka falls instead of hovering), keeps `Action_Antizooka` for charge/muzzle/shell juice, and registers the clone with PEAKLib. Do not replace the vanilla AntiZooka.

Vanilla hold pose, hands, mesh geometry and shaders stay. There is no custom arm IK or mesh swap. `BazookaPaint` recolours the clone only, in the colours of PEAK's firework rocket (the ammo): red fittings, a white tube and muzzle cap, and the grips and spoked exhaust wheel keep the AntiZooka's own tarnished-gold material (the original `Material`, not a copy). The AntiZooka mesh is not CPU-readable, so `MeshReadback` copies its GPU vertex/index buffers byte for byte into a mesh of the mod's own (checked against the original's bounds), split into one submesh per look: the star-material submesh is the tube; of the brass submesh's connected pieces, those reaching below Z −1.2 are the grips and the widest is the wheel. Same vertices and bounds, so the held position cannot change — where an item is held comes only from `Item.defaultPos`. Red and white are copies of the brass material with a flat `_MainTex`, hue shift off and most grime removed. If the read-back fails, the whole brass slot goes red and the tube white. The loaded antigravity `Shell` (the pale disc in the muzzle) has its mesh renderers disabled; its transform and particles stay. Never edit the AntiZooka's shared materials or mesh.

The rocket in flight is `RocketModel`: PEAK's `InertRocket` resource (the rocket pack's spent rocket), stripped of scripts, physics and its firework particles (they drew coloured discs and black smoke around the shooter) under an inactive holder. The model stands upright: its own +Y (the nose) is turned to +Z, the flight direction — its bounds' longest side is not the length, which made it fly nose-up. Scaled to a 0.22m bore. It falls back to the AntiZooka's antigravity `Shell` if the resource is missing.

## Fire

Click-to-fire: `item.usingTimePrimary = 0`, `Action_Gun.OnPressed`, Harmony skip of `Action_Antizooka.PlayChargeFX`. On press, `Action_Gun` asks the host to authorize the shot (fire rate, ammo, still held), then every client spawns a `BazookaRocket`. Everything after the trigger — rocket launch, explosion, blast, every hit RPC — goes through the shooter's `BazookaShooter` (added to every `Character` next to `GunCharacterLaunch`, RPCs on the scout's view), and accepted shots live in the static `ShotLedger`. Never route it through the gun: PEAK destroys a held item when it is put away, which used to delete rockets in flight. The shooter's rocket reports impact (`BazookaRocket` sweeps with `SphereCastNonAlloc` and takes the nearest hit that is not the shooter or an item they hold — a single `SphereCast` stopped at your own arm and flew through point-blank zombies; of the colliders it starts inside, only another character's solid body counts, or it explodes in the muzzle on the held bazooka / trigger zones); `DetonateRocket` plays a dynamite explosion scaled so the fireball matches `ExplosionRadius` (default 3.5m = 0.7 of dynamite's 5m; `ExplosionVolume` live cfg) and runs `BazookaCombat` in that same overlap sphere. The explosion is a visual-only copy of dynamite's prefab (`Plugin.BuildVisualOnlyExplosion`): its `AOE` (dynamite-sized knockback, ragdoll, Injury and item launch in dynamite's own range — the old "nuke") plus colliders and rigidbodies are stripped. Everything the explosion leaves is deleted `ExplosionCleanupSeconds` (7s) after the blast: a `RemoveAfterSeconds` on the explosion root and on `ExplosionEffect.explosionOrb` (a copy under an inactive holder when the orb is its own prefab — the orbs are spawned as separate objects, so removing the explosion never removed them); `PlayExplosion` also `Destroy`s the instance after the same time. All gameplay comes from `BazookaShooter.ApplyBlast`, out to `Plugin.BlastReach` (`ExplosionRadius`, but at least `MinBlastReach` = 6m — a 1.5m hit sphere, the old default, made near misses do nothing): one entry per victim (by character / Photon view, not `transform.root` — terrain shares a scene root) at its nearest collider, and `strength` = 1 − distance / reach scales scout/zombie knockback (`GunCharacterLaunch.Blast`: one velocity change on every bone right after `Fall()`, `Plugin.Knockback` (30 m/s, 40% at the edge) — the shooter's value, sent in `RPC_GunImpact` / `RPC_BazookaBlast`; creature, tumbleweed, Bing Bong and loose-item pushes scale with `KnockbackScale` — a push spread over several steps is eaten by ground friction) and scout Injury (0.4, half at the edge). `PlayExplosion` runs on every client and cooks (`ItemCooking.FinishCooking`) and pushes the ground items that client owns (`BlastLooseItems`), as a vanilla `AOE` with `cooksItems` does. `ExplosionVisualScale` and `ExplosionOneShot` are retired. Self-knockback distance is the nearest ragdoll collider, not `Center`, so a floor shot still launches.

Targets: `[Targets]` has one bool per `ShootableRule` (all on) plus `ScoutmasterHits` (3), host-synced as the `PeakBazooka_Shootables` room bitmask. One rocket = one hit; everything has 1 hit to defeat except the Scoutmaster (earlier rockets call `ShotTarget.PartialHit` → knock-down; the last warps him away with a huge `chillForSeconds`). Zombies are knocked out on the first rocket. Spiders have no vanilla death: `SpiderTarget` lets go, unregisters from `SpiderManager` and deactivates on every client (`BroadcastsDefeat`), and only counts a blast near the spider's body because its trigger is a tall column. `BazookaCombat.BlastPoint` is the blast centre for that check.

The shooter is not a `BazookaCombat` scout hit: `ApplySelfBlast` handles them. `Mode` (host-synced, `BazookaMode`) picks the rules. **Standard**: other scouts get Injury + ragdoll; self-blast is `Fall()` + `SelfKnockback` (distance falloff, extra up) + `SelfDamage` (0.25, same full-to-half curve as `ScoutInjury`). **RocketJump** (was called TF2): scouts are never hit (shooter skips them in `TryHitCharacter`, victim re-checks in `ApplyGunImpact`); creatures still are; no recoil; self-blast is `RocketJump` → `GunCharacterLaunch.BeginRocketFlight`. Old `TF2RocketJump = true` / `SelfRagdoll = false` migrate to `Mode = RocketJump`; `Mode = TF2` is rewritten before `Config.Bind` parses it (`RenameOldModeValue` — an unknown enum name would fall back to Standard), and the `[RocketJump]` keys move to `[RocketJump]` (`MigrateRenamedSetting`). The enum number is unchanged, so the room property still matches.

Rocket flight (`RocketFlight.cs`): PEAK multiplies each bodypart's velocity by `CharacterMovement.drag` (0.85) every physics step while you have ragdoll control, which kills a launch in ~0.1s. While flying, Harmony prefixes on `Bodypart.Drag` / `CharacterItems.AddDrag` keep the shared velocity (`data.avarageVelocity`, capped at 40 m/s) and damp only each rig's motion relative to it; `Bodypart.AddMovementForce` / `CharacterItems.AddMovementForce` become TF2 air acceleration (capped wish speed); `CharacterMovement.GetGravityForce` returns `[RocketJump] Gravity`. Rigs are `ContinuousDynamic` while flying. Each launch (`BeginRocketFlight`) first removes the body's velocity component heading into the blast, then adds the launch: plain addition (TF2's rule) only cancelled a long PEAK fall, so chained floor shots while falling did nothing. Flight ends the moment you are grounded, or on climbing, being carried, ragdoll/pass-out, or 15s. Launch sets `sinceJump = 0` once (like a vanilla jump: `CanStand` needs `sinceJump` > 0.3s, so the floor you blasted off is not a landing); each step holds `sinceGrounded` at ≤ 0.21s (no fall score — `Land` only checks after 0.5s — and no mid-air jump) plus `CapFallDamage(0)`. Never hold `sinceJump` at 0 every step: then `isGrounded` never comes back and you slide around in air physics after touching down. Do not go back to fighting drag with `sinceGrounded` / `isJumping` / forced ragdoll control — that was the old broken TF2 jump. `Plugin.Update` polls `PeakBazooka.cfg` every 0.5s and `Config.Reload()`s — do not cache config at item-clone time.

`Action_Antizooka.RPCA_Shoot` is still sent after the host accepts the shot, but for the bazooka `AntizookaShotJuicePatch` runs `Action_Gun.PlayShotJuice` instead: the same shake and fire SFX, no muzzle flash (the AntiZooka's is a burst of antigravity bubbles; the particle system stays on the clone, it is just never played), and a recoil that settles at the prefab's `defaultPos`. The vanilla juice always settles at (0, 0, 0), which left the bazooka higher after its first shot.

Magazine is `Action_Ammo`. `Shots` default 0 = infinite (`totalUses = -1`). Host-synced. No ammo piles / refill.

## Release

The package is built with `dotnet build Peak.Bazooka.slnx -c Release -target:PackTS` (tcli, `artifacts/thunderstore/`). `<Version>` in the csproj is the package version; bump it only when a release is asked for, together with a `CHANGELOG.md` section for that exact version (`.cursor/rules/changelog-on-release.mdc`). Keep the PEAKLib versions in `thunderstore.toml` equal to the csproj `PackageReference`s (the workflow checks). The package identity (namespace, name) lives only in `thunderstore.toml`; the workflow reads it from there. The repo must build from a fresh clone, with PEAK installed or with `-p:ManagedDir=` pointing at PEAKGameLibs' `package/lib/` — keep README's Build and Make your own version sections true when moving things. Screenshots are `media/img1.jpg` … `img3.jpg`, shown at the top of both `README.md` and `THUNDERSTORE.md` through raw GitHub URLs on `master` (Thunderstore needs absolute URLs), so they appear once pushed. `.github/workflows/thunderstore.yml` runs on every push to `master`: it builds the zip and publishes to Thunderstore (FakeGameDevelopers) when `<Version>` is not live yet, so **pushing a version bump to master is a release**. Bump `<Version>` only when a release is asked for.

## Do not

- Reintroduce a custom mesh / `HoldPose` config / hand-anchor solver unless the user asks for a new model
- `Destroy` ParticleSystems on the AntiZooka clone (the muzzle flash lives there; the bazooka simply never plays it)
- `PhotonNetwork.Instantiate` the AntiSphere prefab from the clone
- Instantiate dynamite's raw `explosionPrefab` — its `AOE` hits everyone in dynamite range; use `Plugin.ExplosionPrefab` (visual only)
- Leave `Antigrav` on the clone (that is why a dropped AntiZooka floats)
